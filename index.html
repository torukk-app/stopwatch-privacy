Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Developer: torukk
Application: Easy! Stopwatch & Timer (Android; package name: com.torukk.stopwatch)

torukk provides Easy! Stopwatch & Timer (the “App”) free of charge. The App includes advertising provided through Google AdMob. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored by the App and what information may be processed by third-party services.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores stopwatch and timer information, including saved measurements, settings, and timing state, in local app storage so that its features work. The developer does not operate a server to receive or maintain your saved stopwatch measurements. The App does not require you to create an account.

Depending on your device and Android settings, local app data may also be included in operating-system backup or restore services, which are managed separately by the device or platform provider.

2. Information processed by advertising and consent services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage applicable advertising privacy notices and choices. These Google services may automatically collect, process, or share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • Device or account-related identifiers, including an advertising identifier where available;
  • App interactions, such as app launches and interactions relevant to advertising;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as errors and app/SDK performance; and
  • Advertising consent and privacy-choice information, as applicable.

These data may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, support analytics related to advertising, improve service performance, and detect fraud or abuse. Processing depends on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's policies. The App does not request precise GPS location for its stopwatch or timer functions.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

3. Sharing of information

Information described in Section 2 may be transmitted to or processed by Google and its advertising partners in connection with advertising and consent management. The developer does not independently send users' saved stopwatch or timer measurements to an external server or sell those measurements.

4. Your choices

Where required and available, the App displays privacy or consent options provided by UMP. You can revisit applicable choices through the App's “Privacy settings” menu when that option is shown. You may also manage advertising-related preferences in your Android or Google settings. Some advertisements may still be displayed depending on applicable settings and choices.

5. Data retention and deletion

Saved stopwatch/timer information remains in the App's local storage until you delete the relevant records, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Device backups may follow separate retention and deletion rules established by the operating-system or backup provider.

Information processed by Google or its advertising partners is subject to their respective retention and deletion practices. Please refer to the linked Google policies for information about those practices and your controls. For privacy questions or requests relating to the developer's handling of data, please contact the email address below.

6. Security

We use platform-provided app storage and rely on the security measures of the third-party services integrated into the App. Network transmissions handled by Google services use their applicable security protections. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. External links

The App may contain links to third-party websites, services, or applications. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and torukk is not responsible for those external services.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children. torukk does not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children. If you have concerns about a child's information in relation to the App, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality, integrated services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy and effective date will be published on this page.

本アプリは、ストップウォッチ・タイマーの計測結果、設定、計測状態などを機能の提供のために端末内のアプリ領域へ保存します。開発者は、保存された計測記録を受信・保管する独自のサーバーを運営していません。本アプリの利用にアカウント登録は必要ありません。

2. 広告・同意管理サービスによる情報の処理

本アプリは、広告表示のためにGoogle Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）を、広告に関するプライバシー通知や選択肢の管理のためにGoogle User Messaging Platform（UMP）SDKを利用しています。これらのGoogleのサービスは、以下の情報を自動的に収集・処理・共有する場合があります。

  • IPアドレス（おおよその位置の推定に使用される場合があります）
  • 広告IDなどの端末・アカウントに関連する識別子
  • アプリの起動や広告に関連する操作などの利用情報
  • エラー、アプリやSDKの動作状況などの診断・パフォーマンス情報

これらの情報は、広告の配信・効果測定、広告関連の分析、サービス改善、不正行為の防止などに利用されます。実際の処理は、端末の設定、居住地域、同意の選択、Googleのポリシーなどによって異なります。本アプリは、ストップウォッチ・タイマー機能のためにGPSによる正確な位置情報を要求しません。

詳細は、GoogleのプライバシーポリシーおよびGoogleのサービスを使用するサイトやアプリから収集した情報の使用をご参照ください。
